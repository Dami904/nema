The method

Three quiet steps

No fund sees another's number. Nobody sees the result without paying for it.

01

Seal

Each fund encrypts its mark in the browser to the round's key. Only the enclave can open it.

02

Tally

At the deadline the enclave counts, averages and measures the spread. The chain gets a count and a commitment.

03

Deliver

A lender pays and receives the result sealed to their own key, with a salt to check it against the chain.
